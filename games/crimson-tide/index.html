❔ How to Play

Goal: Build a base, raise an army, and destroy every enemy force.

Economy: Build a Power Plant, then an Ore Refinery (comes with a Harvester). Harvesters mine golden ore and convert it to credits. Keep power positive or production slows.

Tech tree: Refinery → War Factory → Tech Lab unlocks heavy units & advanced defenses.

Combat: Units have armor types (infantry / vehicle / air). Weapons deal bonus or reduced damage by type — pair the right counters. Units gain veterancy from kills (▴ Veteran, ▴▴ Elite).

Controls: WASD/arrows or screen edges scroll · Left-drag box-select · Right-click move/attack/harvest · Q select army · F attack-move · X stop · P pause · Esc cancel. Click a build card, then click the map to place a structure.

Factions: Allies (prism & stealth), Soviet (heavy armor & tesla), Empire (fast mechs & wave-force).
